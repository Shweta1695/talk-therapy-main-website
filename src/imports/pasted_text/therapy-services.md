CREATING A RELATIONSHIP YOU ALWAYS DREAMT OF HAVING
Couples typically enter a cycle where they get disconnected, they fight, and no matter what the topic is, the conflict seems to go the same way and end up in the same place - where we feel unheard, misunderstood, or alone.
Emotionally Focused Couple Therapy helps partners recognize these patterns, understand what happens when communication breaks down, and express their deeper emotions and needs in ways that allow them to deeply hear and respond to each other.

THE POWER OF EMOTIONAL CONNECTION
Across all meaningful relationships—romantic, familial, social, and professional—we share a fundamental need for emotional connection. 
Emotional connection is the experience of feeling safe, seen, understood, and responded to by another person. It creates a secure bond that supports our basic human needs for belonging, acceptance, comfort, and significance.

HEALING THE PAST, THE FUTURE AND THE PRESENT

Attachment and emotional connection
From an Emotionally Focused Therapy perspective, attachment is less about placing you into fixed “styles” and more about understanding how you seek closeness, respond to emotional distance, and protect yourself when connection feels uncertain. These patterns often develop as understandable ways of managing vulnerability, rejection, conflict, or the fear of not being emotionally met.
We may explore your family of origin to better understand what you learned about emotions, relationships, and emotional safety. Early experiences can shape how comfortable you feel expressing needs, receiving support, trusting others, and creating emotional connection.
This exploration is not about blaming the past, but about making sense of the patterns that continue to shape your relationships today.
By bringing awareness and compassion to these patterns, therapy can help you communicate more openly, respond less defensively, and build relationships that feel safer, more secure, and emotionally connected.


Anxiety and depression
Anxiety, chronic stress, and depression can leave you feeling overwhelmed, disconnected, or unsure of how to move forward. Therapy can help you understand the emotional patterns, experiences, and underlying needs contributing to your distress. 
Together, we will use practical coping strategies, somatic approaches, and psychological reframing to help you feel more grounded, regain a sense of direction, and create meaningful, lasting changes to develop healthier ways of responding to life’s challenges.

WORKING WITH YOUR INNER WORLD
We all develop different parts of ourselves in response to our experiences and relationships. A vulnerable or younger part may carry fear, sadness, shame, or unmet needs, while protective parts may try to keep us safe through avoidance, control, withdrawal, anger, perfectionism or self-criticism.
Through attachment-informed and experiential parts work, we will approach all parts with curiosity and compassion. The goal is not to eliminate them, but to understand what they are protecting and what they need. Over time, your grounded adult self can learn to respond to your emotions and needs with greater safety, confidence, and care.

OUR SERVICES

INDIVIDUAL THERAPY
In individual therapy, you will be met with curiosity, honesty, and compassion. Together, we will explore your inner emotional world, including the early attachment wounds and protective patterns that may still influence how you feel, relate, and respond today. I will help you slow down, understand what lies beneath your reactions, and develop practical strategies for creating the life and relationships you want.
Change may begin with a deeper understanding of yourself, but over time it becomes something you can feel: more grounded, less controlled by old patterns, and more able to respond to your emotions and needs with clarity, confidence, and care.
COUPLES THERAPY
In couples therapy, I create a space where both partners can feel heard without turning therapy into a search for who is right or wrong. Together, we will slow down the cycle that keeps pulling you into the same conflict and begin to see the cycle—not each other—as the problem. I will help you understand what is happening emotionally between you and express what is often hidden beneath anger, withdrawal, criticism, or defensiveness.
Change occurs as familiar reactions begin to soften and each partner becomes more emotionally accessible and responsive. Conversations can start to feel safer, conflict less threatening, and the relationship more like a place of trust, closeness, and support.

A supportive space to repair communication, rebuild trust, and strengthen emotional connection. Learn to move beyond recurring conflicts, understand each other more deeply, and create a more secure and fulfilling partnership.

HOW WE WILL WORK TOGETHER
Meaningful change begins within a trusting therapeutic relationship. My approach is collaborative, compassionate, and non-judgmental. I will meet you where you are, help you feel understood, and create a space where difficult emotions and experiences can be explored safely and honestly.
Building on Your Strengths
You already possess strengths, insights, and inner resources that can support meaningful change. Together, we will identify what matters most to you, understand what may be keeping you stuck, and clarify the life and relationships you want to create. Rather than imposing a predetermined direction, our work will be guided by your needs, values, and goals.
Emotionally Focused and Relational Care
Our experiences of connection, loss, conflict, and belonging shape how we understand ourselves and relate to others. Through emotion-focused, relational, and mindfulness-based approaches, I will help you slow down, understand what is happening beneath familiar reactions, and make space for the emotions and needs that may be difficult to express.
Creating Change in the Present
Although we may explore your past to understand how certain patterns developed, therapy will also focus on how those patterns are affecting your life today. By developing greater awareness and practising new ways of responding in the present, you can begin to heal what continues to hurt, move through challenges with greater clarity, and shape a future that feels more purposeful, connected, and aligned with who you want to be.

ABOUT ME
II've lived in several countries, speak four languages, and have called Canada home for the past 17 years. My path into psychology is actually my second career — and that experience deeply shaped the way I practice today.
In my first career, I learned how to build strong, meaningful relationships by understanding how people communicate, what they need, and how they experience the world. Wanting to deepen that understanding, I returned to school and completed a Bachelor of Arts in Human Relations (Concordia University, 2010), where I focused on emotional awareness, interpersonal dynamics, and communication styles.
After graduating, I began specializing in co-parenting and couples work, facilitating workshops that helped partners communicate more effectively and parent as a team. Through that work, I realized I wanted more clinical tools to support individuals and couples at a deeper level. This led me to complete my Master of Arts in Counselling Psychology (McGill University, 2015).
Both during and after my Master's degree, I developed a strong focus on anxiety and depression — challenges I was seeing frequently within the university and broader community. My clinical approach is grounded in Emotion-Focused Therapy (Levels 1 & 2, 2016–2018; Externship, 2022) and Emotion-Focused Couples Therapy (EFCT, 2020), alongside mindfulness-based approaches. I am also trained in Emotion-Focused Mindfulness Therapy (EFMT, 2023) and hold certification in Mindfulness Meditation (2018–2019).
Today, I offer Emotion-Focused Therapy for individuals and couples who want to better understand their emotions, reduce anxiety and depression, and build healthier, more secure relationships. I am especially passionate about helping couples strengthen their connection and create lasting change.

Therapy is not about “fixing” you — it is about creating a space where you can understand yourself more clearly, process what you’ve been carrying, and move forward with greater ease and connection.
